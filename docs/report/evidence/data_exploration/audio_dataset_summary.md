## Dataset observations

- The manifest records one environment (`quiet`) and one noise condition (`clean`) for all 125 primary clips; the only degraded conditions are the four derived clips (gain reduced to −52 dBFS; white noise at 5 dB SNR).
- 96% of clips are synthetic macOS voices; accent and spontaneous-speech variation is limited to one human speaker with five clips.
- Human clips are on average about twice as long as the TTS renderings of comparable phrases (3.89 s vs 2.00 s).
- Every file is 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM, matching `audio_sample_rate` in `configs/settings.py`, so no resampling occurs on these clips.
