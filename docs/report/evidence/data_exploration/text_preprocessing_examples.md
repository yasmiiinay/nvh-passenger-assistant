## MiniLM tokenisation of two normalised queries

Tokenizer: `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (WordPiece, uncased; `##` marks a word continuation).

- `h004` input: `i have desk 225 on my ticket which terminal is that`  
  tokens (11 for 11 words): `i have desk 225 on my ticket which terminal is that`  
  every word maps to one WordPiece token.
- `q036` input: `what gate is flight xy456 leaving from`  
  tokens (10 for 7 words): `what gate is flight x ##y ##45 ##6 leaving from`  
  split into sub-word pieces: `xy456` → `x ##y ##45 ##6`.

Numeric identifiers that exist as whole tokens in the vocabulary survive intact, while alphanumeric codes are fragmented; in both cases the embedding carries no notion of an identifier *range*, which is why the cascade resolves identifiers deterministically against the KB before any embedding is computed.
